Engineering Process

From Technical CAD Blueprint to Physical Reality

Architectural mastery lies in bridging the gap between exact 2D dimensional drafting and 3D physical construction. Explore how we translate drafting table blueprints into physical landmarks.

01
Site Analysis & Vastu Spatial Orientation
We conduct precise laser boundary measurements, soil test evaluation, and analyze sun-path angles to establish the building footprint.
02
Schematic Floor Plans & Volumetric 3D Model
Room dimensions, ceiling heights, cross-ventilation, and physical scale models are developed to validate spatial flow.
03
Structural Engineering & Reinforcement Sets
Every column, footing depth, cantilever beam, and rebar gauge is calculated to withstand seismic loads with zero settlement risks.
04
On-Site Execution Oversight & Handover
Our principal architects inspect formwork, concrete slump tests, and joinery alignment at every critical milestone.
