Frequently Asked Questions

Consultation & Project FAQs

Everything you need to know about starting an architectural or interior design project with A&A in Meerut.

Do you charge for preliminary site visits in Meerut?

No. We offer complimentary preliminary site assessments across Ganga Nagar, Mawana Road, Radha Garden, Shastri Nagar, and central Meerut to understand your space and project scope.

Do you provide 3D visual walkthroughs before execution?

Yes. Our architectural team produces photorealistic 3D elevations and spatial perspectives for every room so you can visualize the false ceiling, lighting, veneers, and furniture before construction begins.

Can I hire you for single-room makeovers like kitchen or bedroom?

Absolutely. While we specialize in complete turnkey villa and commercial interior architecture, we also execute specialized single-room projects such as modular kitchens or false ceilings.

What is the typical execution timeline for a 3BHK in Meerut?

Depending on customization and civil alterations, a full turnkey 3BHK interior is typically delivered within 45 to 60 days, backed by our punctual delivery guarantee.
