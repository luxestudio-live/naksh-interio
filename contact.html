Help & Clarity

Consultation & Site Assessment FAQs

What should I bring to my first architectural consultation?

Bring your plot registry or dimensions, any survey maps, and a list of your family’s room requirements. If you have inspirational images, our architects can incorporate them into your custom schematic design.

Can you assist with building sanction approvals in Meerut?

Yes. We prepare complete municipal sanction drawings complying with Meerut Development Authority (MDA) and UP Awas Vikas bye-laws, including FAR, ground coverage, and setback regulations.

Do you handle both architectural design and full construction?

Yes. We offer both Design-Only commissions (providing all blueprints, 3D views, and structural CAD) and Full Turnkey Design-Build execution where we manage civil contractors, materials, and handover.

Do you execute projects in Mawana, Modipuram, and surrounding UP areas?

Yes. While our studio is based in Ganga Nagar, Meerut, we actively design and supervise residential villas and commercial spaces across Mawana Road, Modipuram, Shastri Nagar, Hapur, and NCR.
