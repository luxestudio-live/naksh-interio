Frequently Asked Questions

Architectural & Interior Queries

What is the difference between an architect and an interior designer?

An architect plans the entire physical structure from foundation to elevation, ensuring structural safety, BDA bye-laws, natural lighting, and site integration. An interior designer crafts the internal living spaces, joinery, and lighting. Kunj Architects combines both under one roof for seamless synergy.

Can you handle building sanction approvals with Bareilly Development Authority (BDA)?

Yes. We prepare complete municipal sanction drawings complying with Bareilly Development Authority (BDA) and UP Awas Vikas bye-laws, including FAR, ground coverage, and setback regulations.

Do you execute turnkey projects outside Bareilly?

Yes, while our flagship studio is located in Ramganga Nagar, Dohra Road, Bareilly, we actively design and execute luxury villas and turnkey residences across Dohra Road, Civil Lines, Ramganga Nagar, Dohra Road, Pilibhit, Rampur, and UP.

How do you ensure material quality during turnkey execution?

We use strict BOQ specifications naming certified brands (e.g., Century/Green HDHMR, Hafele/Blum hardware, Asian Paints Royal, Saint-Gobain glass) and our Principal Architect conducts physical audits before any material is installed.
