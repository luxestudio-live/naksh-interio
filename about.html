Our Founding Vision

Elevating Meerut’s residential & commercial architecture.

Founded in Ganga Nagar, Meerut, A&A Architect & Interior Designer was established to bridge the gap between high-level architectural spatial engineering and master-crafted interior execution. We believe extraordinary design should not be reserved only for multi-crore metropolitan projects; it should be accessible, affordable, and executed with uncompromising integrity right here in Uttar Pradesh.

From independent luxury villas in Radha Garden and Mawana Road to modern apartments in Shastri Nagar and corporate offices along Delhi Road, our studio delivers turnkey precision that respects client budgets while delivering magazine-worthy living spaces.

Master Craftsmanship

Dedicated carpentry, false ceilings & modular joinery.

Great interior design is won in the details. That is why our team includes seasoned civil engineers, gypsum artisans, and master carpenters trained in boiling-water-proof (BWP) HDHMR joinery, acoustic fluted timber cladding, and German soft-close fittings.

Every false ceiling profile, concealed cove illumination channel, and custom quartz countertop is measured with laser precision and manufactured to withstand the test of time, humidity, and daily family life.

The A&A Standard

Our 4 Architectural Pillars

Every project undertaken by our studio is governed by four core commitments to quality, transparency, and architectural finesse.

01

Scientific Space Planning

We eliminate dead zones and optimize natural sunlight, ventilation, and ergonomic flow through comprehensive 2D layouts and photorealistic 3D perspectives.

02

Material Integrity

Only certified HDHMR substrates, Statuario marble slabs, German hardware runners, and anti-scratch acrylic finishes enter our client residences.

03

Transparent Budgeting

We provide clear, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with affordable rates and zero surprise additions, keeping your interior investment safe and predictable.

04

Single-Point Supervision

From day-one demolition to final white-glove handover, our lead architects personally audit every millimeter on site for total peace of mind.
