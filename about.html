Chapter 01 · Architecture

Shaping the Built Skyline of Bareilly & NCR

Founded in Ramganga Nagar, Bareilly, Kunj Architects was created to eliminate the traditional divide between architects who stop at paper blueprints and interior decorators who cannot alter structural geometry.

Our architectural wing designs contemporary residential villas, commercial hubs, and luxury duplexes engineered with cantilevered concrete slabs, natural travertine stone facades, thermal solar louvers, and municipal sanction compliance under Bareilly Development Authority (BDA) bye-laws.

Chapter 02 · Interior Architecture

Master joinery, boiling-water-proof HDHMR & marble craft.

Inside every building, we believe spaces should feel calm, sensory, and enduring. That is why our interior studio employs master joiners and finishes artisans trained in German soft-close tandem hardware, boiling-water-proof (BWP) HDHMR joinery, and book-matched Italian marble walls.

Every false ceiling cove, backlit CNC laser jali, and quartz kitchen island is calculated with laser accuracy to endure decades of humidity, temperature shifts, and daily Indian family living.

The Kunj Standard

Our 4 Practice Commitments

Every architectural commission and turnkey interior project is governed by four uncompromising standards.

01

Structural & Vastu Precision

We eliminate dead circulation and harmonize solar paths, wind directions, and Vastu principles through comprehensive structural CAD sets and 3D modeling.

02

Material Authenticity

Only certified HDHMR substrates, authentic Statuario marble slabs, Hafele/Blum hardware, and non-porous quartz countertops are permitted on our client sites.

03

Transparent Itemized BOQ

We provide clear, detailed Bill of Quantities with zero hidden fees and locked material grades, safeguarding client investments from cost escalations.

04

Turnkey Direct Oversight

From initial soil excavation and formwork casting to final move-in handover, our Principal Architect and Senior Leads personally inspect every milestone.
